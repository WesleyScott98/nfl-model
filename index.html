How it works

The big number is the chance the bet wins. 75% hits about three times in four — and still loses the fourth.

Order comes from the model alone: how likely a bet is, and how far its projection clears the number. Sportsbook odds don't affect the ranking.

"Worth about −150" is the break-even price. If FanDuel pays better than that, good deal. Worse, and you're overpaying even on a likely bet.

"50+ rushing yards" means he goes over 50.

FanDuel's price is shown next to the model's fair price when it's available. A VALUE badge means FanDuel is paying more than the bet is worth — those are the ones worth taking.

Nothing shorter than −200 is listed as a single bet. At −200 you risk two units to win one, which is about the limit worth paying on a single bet. Short prices are still fine as parlay legs — that's what the Parlays tab is for.

Spreads and totals are the hardest bets to beat, so the model tracks FanDuel there instead of guessing. Player bets are where it has an opinion.

When this page updates. It rebuilds itself on a schedule — nobody presses a button:

Wed evening — Thursday night game, with FanDuel prop prices

Sat 1pm — the whole Sunday slate, with FanDuel prop prices

Sun evening — Monday night game, with FanDuel prop prices

~90 minutes before each kickoff — a final check of who's playing, plus refreshed spreads, totals and moneylines

Why props land a day early rather than constantly: pulling prop prices costs money per game, so they're pulled once per slate, about a day out — which is when most people bet. Until then a bet shows the model's fair price only.

Injuries. Three checks, so a hurt player doesn't show up as a pick:

1. The official injury report (published Wed–Fri). Anyone listed Out or Doubtful is removed and his workload goes to teammates.

2. Live status, rechecked on every update including the one ~90 minutes before kickoff. If a player is ruled out late, he drops off at the next update.

3. An early-warning check — anyone whose playing time collapsed last week is treated as a coin flip to play, before it's official.

Still worth a glance: a player listed questionable is simulated as 50/50, not removed. If he's scratched minutes before kickoff, this page may not have caught it yet. Check the app before you bet.

Where the numbers come from. Every game is simulated 20,000 times using play-by-play data from the last three seasons: how often each player is used, how his team plays, who's hurt, who's inactive, and the weather.

How good is it? Tested on the 2024 and 2025 seasons, it beats a naive guess by roughly 30% on catches and yards, and when it says 70%, those bets win about 70% of the time. It is not a crystal ball. Bet accordingly.
